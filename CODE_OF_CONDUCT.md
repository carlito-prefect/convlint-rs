# Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose
This project is committed to providing a professional, respectful, and harassment-free environment for all contributors and participants, regardless of personal background, identity, or level of experience.

## 2. Expected Standards
All participants are expected to:
* Conduct themselves professionally and courteously in all project spaces.
* Communicate constructively and accept feedback graciously.
* Prioritize the collaborative integrity and security of the community.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct
Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:
* Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
* The dissemination of sexualized content or inappropriate advances.
* The unauthorized publication of private personal data (doxxing).
* Any form of disruptive behavior that undermines a productive environment.

## 4. Scope and Enforcement
This policy applies to all project repositories, issue trackers, and official communications.

Instances of non-compliance may be reported confidentially to the maintainers.
Project maintainers hold full authority to edit or remove content, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently suspend access to project resources for any violations.
